02 — Selected work

What I built this year

Three projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

The Galactic Ruins of Aravia

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 9 weeks

The Galactic Ruins of Aravia is a 3D, third person, single player A to B games where the player will at the start of the maps and they must work there way through enemies and obsticales at every turn.

My role: everything — gameplay classes, spawning, scoring, HUD, the end-of-round state, the enemy AI and the world design.

Santa's escape

Unreal Engine 5 · Level design · Solo · 4 weeks

Santa escape is a 2D christmas themed game where santa must survive a uprising from the evil ginger bread men who are depmanding better pay for making all of santas present, you must escape as santa with all the presents to ensure all the children have there presents for christmas.

My role: layout, world design , piskel character design and all the blueprint coding.

Undead Hills

Unreal engine 5 · adobe aftereffects · partnership · 16 weeks

Eight modular low-poly props under 1,500 triangles each, UV unwrapped, textured, and imported into Unreal with correct scale and collision.

My role: modelling, UVs, texturing and engine import.

Footsteps and Foley

Reaper · Unreal MetaSounds · Solo · 1 week

A surface-aware footstep system: recorded foley on four surfaces, layered and randomised so the same step never plays twice in a row.

My role: recording, editing, and implementation in engine.

Skyline

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 2 weeks

A 2D platformer prototype exploring coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height — the small details that decide whether a jump feels good.

My role: movement system, camera, and level.

48-Hour Game Jam: Signal Lost

Unreal Engine 5 · Team of 3 · 48 hours

A first game jam, built to the theme "no way back". Finished and submitted with ten minutes to spare, which taught me more about scoping than any lecture has.

My role: gameplay programming and build packaging.

03 — About

About me

My name is Adam Johnson, and I am a first-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh Campus. My passion for games began through playing them, exploring their worlds and taking them apart to understand how they work behind the scenes. This curiosity inspired me to pursue Games Development and learn how to create games of my own. I have a particular interest in world and level design, where I enjoy imagining immersive environments, designing interesting locations and creating experiences that encourage players to explore and engage with the game. I am developing my skills in game design, creativity and technical development, with the ambition of becoming a professional game designer and contributing to the creation of exciting, memorable gaming experiences.

I am particularly interested in world and level design, where I can use my creativity to build immersive environments and engaging spaces for players to explore. I enjoy designing interesting locations, thinking about how levels flow and creating worlds that feel believable and memorable. Throughout my first year, I have been developing my skills in C++ and Blueprints, modelling and texturing my own assets, and recording and implementing my own audio. These experiences have helped me understand how different elements of game development come together to create a complete and enjoyable player experience.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project.
