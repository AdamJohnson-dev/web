02 — Selected work

What I built this year

Three projects from first year. Each one lists the tools I used and what I was responsible for.

The Galactic Ruins of Aravia

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 9 weeks

A 3D, third-person, single-player A-to-B game. The player starts at one end of the map and must work their way through enemies and obstacles at every turn.

My role: everything — gameplay classes, spawning, scoring, HUD, the end-of-round state, the enemy AI and the world design.

Santa's Escape

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Piskel · Solo · 4 weeks

A 2D Christmas-themed game where Santa must survive an uprising from the evil gingerbread men, who are demanding better pay for making all of Santa's presents. Escape with every present to make sure all the children have their gifts for Christmas.

My role: layout, world design, Piskel character design and all the Blueprint coding.

Undead Hills

Unreal Engine 5 · Adobe After Effects · Team of two · 16 weeks

A 3D first-person shooter set in a post-apocalyptic world where humans are turning into zombies en masse. Fight through the zombie patrols with very limited ammo and equipment to secure the antidote at the top of the king's castle.

My role: world and level design, some Blueprint coding and lighting.

03 — About

About me

My name is Adam Johnson, and I am a first-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh Campus. My passion for games began through playing them, exploring their worlds and taking them apart to understand how they work behind the scenes. That curiosity is what pushed me to learn how to make games of my own.

I am particularly interested in world and level design: imagining immersive environments, thinking about how a level flows, and building places that feel believable and make players want to explore. Throughout first year I have been developing my skills in C++ and Blueprints, modelling and texturing my own assets, and recording and implementing my own audio, which has shown me how the different parts of development come together into a complete player experience.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project, with the ambition of becoming a professional game designer.
